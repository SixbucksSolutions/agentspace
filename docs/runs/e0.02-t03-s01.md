# E0.02-T03-S01 run record: a real project runs on a lease, unattended

RFC 2119 keywords; the capitals are load-bearing.

The evidence for card #0060, v0.02's done-when run (epic #0045), gathered live. Part 1 ran on the
evening of 2026-11-17, laptop on Pacific time (-08:00); it was parked before Terry's click because
he was away from the laptop, and part 2 resumes with a fresh lease. Times are UTC with `Z`.

- **This record MUST NOT carry the account ID, the instance ID, the box's host name or the Bot
  token.** The box is written `<box>` and the instance `<instance>`.
- **A push MUST be attributed by its sshd `Accepted` line** (session number and certificate `ID`),
  never by a time window alone. In part 1, a time window alone misattributed one push to the
  observer's login; the sshd lines showed it was the lease's.

### The observer

  prints a self-test at start; the self-test found 10 lines in a window known to hold logins.
  `-u ssh` alone found only 6 of those 10.
