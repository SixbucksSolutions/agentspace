### The two tests, their waits and the lines that changed

- **`a_later_holder_is_not_refused_while_an_earlier_one_still_has_the_log`**, in `crates/agentspace-launch/tests/spec_log_handle.rs`.
  - **Before:** the later holder was a stand-in that exits at once (`stub_exit`, old line 147), the start's result was
    allowed to be `Started::Failed` when its text contained `exited at once`, and **the test then polled the log for 10 seconds
    until two `stub-start pid=` lines appeared (old line 165)**. The test's first poll, for the first holder's
    first two ticks, waits 20 seconds at old line 135, and it is unchanged (new line 137).
  - **After:** the later holder is a stand-in that stays running (`stub_sleep`, new line 149), `holder_of` requires the start to
    give a holder, and **the poll waits up to 30 seconds for that holder's own first line, found by its PID (new line 158)**.
    A doc comment on the test says why.
  - **The cause:** the stand-in that exited at once raced step 7 of the handshake (keeper.md). **Caught while it exited, its command
    line could not be read back, and the start failed saying it runs unrecorded** (`The holder started, but its command line could
    not be read back to check it, so it runs unrecorded.`), a message that does not contain `exited at once`, so the test's own
    allowance did not cover it: the failure was at old line 160. It is **a race of the test's stand-in against the start's
    non-atomic read, not a wait that ran out**.
- **`win_tests::a_stop_ends_the_whole_tree_and_leaves_another_holder_with_the_same_command`**, in
  `crates/agentspace-keeper-win/src/win_tests.rs`.
  - **Before:** each tree came from `Holder::wait_for_tree` (old line 131, a 20-second poll) over `descendants`, **which lists the
    processes whose parent PID is the holder's, by parent PID alone**, and the test's check after the stop, that every process of
    the tree is gone (old line 310, `A process of its tree still runs`), runs at once, with no wait.
  - **After:** the test reads each tree by keeper.md's own rule for a holder's tree ("Stopping a holder"), in two new functions,
    **`tree_by_the_stops_rule` (new line 301)**, which counts a process of the tree only when, on its own handle, it still runs,
    gives its parent's PID and was created no earlier than the parent, with every handle held until the walk ends, and
    **`wait_for_tree_by_the_stops_rule` (new line 342, its 30-second deadline at line 343)**, and the test calls it for both
    holders (new lines 362 and 363). The other tests that use `wait_for_tree` (new lines 390 and 437) are unchanged. **These new
    lines are `c585c70`'s; at S05's head `51ef89d`**, which puts 18 more lines before the test, from line 354 ([below](#the-last-item-of-0268-the-check-after-the-stop-polls)),
    lines 301, 342 and 343 are the same, the two calls are lines 380 and 381, and the other uses of `wait_for_tree` lines 416 and 463.
  - **The cause:** on a busy laptop a process whose parent died lists that PID still, and once Windows gave the PID to a holder
    of this test, the orphan looked like the holder's child. The stop, which is right, left it running, and the test's check at
    old line 310 failed. **A race in the test's own tree, not a defect of the stop**, and not a wait that ran out.

## The lessons

**These are the turn's lessons for the architect's playbook** ([architect.md](../architect.md)), named where they belong and not edited into it by this story; the one

- **A refusal whose own text names the allowed form is re-sent in that form, and a "don't retry" refusal is never re-sent.** S04's implementer was refused on both
  of its edits with an answer that said not to retry, and **it stopped**, which is right: #0264 went to Needs Terry. **This writer's own compound `git` call was
