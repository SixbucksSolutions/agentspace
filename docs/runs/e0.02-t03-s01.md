- **This record MUST NOT carry the account ID, the instance ID, the box's host name or the Bot

### The observer

  recorded every 15 seconds who held 127.0.0.1:8811 and :53147 (`sudo ss -ltnp`), sshd's

- **It ends by itself:** at a 2-hour deadline, or when `/home/ubuntu/.agentspace-t03-observer-stop`

  prints a self-test at start; the self-test found 10 lines in a window known to hold logins.
  `-u ssh` alone found only 6 of those 10.
