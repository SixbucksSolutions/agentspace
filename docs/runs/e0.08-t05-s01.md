## The architect's rulings for this turn

run. Each is given in the architect's wording, trimmed, never changed in meaning.

   seconds. A running host whose project holds no live lease is stopped 15 minutes after the later of
   its `LaunchTime` and the project's last lease end (D56; operator.md, guard). So each project's
   lease MUST be taken within 15 minutes of `sync -All` starting its host. A guard stop of a host with
   no live lease is designed behaviour: record it, it is not a finding. A guard stop of a host under a
   live lease IS a finding. Each lease's TTL MUST cover its step with margin, because the idle check
   powers a host off at its horizon plus 15 minutes; #0163's wait can take up to 30 minutes. The
   8-hour default is fine.
