### How the figures the tool does not give were computed

Every figure in the table of the figures comes from the footprint tool's output. The others come
from a short Python over the JSONL, run on the copy:
- **Times of the peaks:** the sample at which each figure peaks, read from the rows.
- **CPU-seconds,** over the session's window (seq 99 to 3148): the difference of `cpu_busy_s`
  (host), `sampler_cpu_s`, `self.cpu_s` and `tools.cpu_s` between the window's first and last sample.
- **Counts of intervals,** by 16 times `cpu_busy_s` over `cpu_total_s` for each 2.0-second step, the
  footprint tool's own host formula.
- **The per-window table:** sums of the tool and self CPU differences over the steps ending in each
  window, with the one negative step clamped to 0, and the maxima of the other columns.
- **The quiet stretch,** by the rises of the session's `tools.cpu_s` and the rows whose `tools.procs`
  is 0.
- **The gap check,** as in v0.05-T01's record: per-step host busy time, less the sampler's, the
  session's own and tool CPU and the baseline, summed over 30-step windows.
- **Ratios in the comparison table,** from the unrounded peaks over the other turns' printed peaks.

### The identifier scan

- **The record's fields:** the start line has `run`, `match`, `cpus`, `mem_total_kib`, `interval_s`,
